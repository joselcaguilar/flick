# Gesture glyphs

Custom SF Symbols-like SVG gesture glyphs for Flick. They use a 24×24 viewBox, 1.75 px rounded strokes, `currentColor`, and no external icon font. Motion gestures can animate their stroke/trail in app code; static outlines are the Reduce Motion source of truth.

Included: point, circle clockwise/counter-clockwise, two-hand separate, thumbs up/down, pinch dial, swipes in four directions, and open palm.
